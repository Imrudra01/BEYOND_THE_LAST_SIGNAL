Opportunity functioned as a robotic field geologist: its Panoramic Mast scanned craters from afar while its 5-jointed Robotic Arm (IDD) placed spectrometers, a microscopic lens, and a diamond grinder directly onto Martian bedrock.

2 MAST SENSORS 4 ARM INSTRUMENTS
MAST • STEREO OPTICS 1024×1024 CCD

01. Pancam (Panoramic Camera)

Two high-resolution digital cameras mounted 1.5 metres high with an 8-filter color wheel on each eye, capturing true-color and multispectral 360-degree panoramas.

★ Discovery: Imaged layered bedrock at Eagle Crater & the final darkened Sun on Sol 5,111.
MAST • THERMAL IR 5–29 μm IR

02. Mini-TES Spectrometer

Miniature Thermal Emission Spectrometer saw in infrared wavelengths through dust coatings to map water-formed minerals (hematite, sulfates, and silicates) from afar.

★ Discovery: Mapped wide plains of gray crystalline hematite across Meridiani Planum.
ROBOTIC ARM • TURRET Fe-MINERALOGY

03. Mössbauer Spectrometer (MB)

Pinpointed iron-bearing minerals in Martian rocks and soil using gamma-ray resonance, distinguishing volcanic iron from minerals formed in liquid water.

★ Discovery: Proved the "blueberries" were hematite (Fe₂O₃) & identified water-formed jarosite.
ROBOTIC ARM • TURRET X-RAY / ALPHA

04. Alpha Particle X-Ray (APXS)

Placed directly against outcrops to measure elemental chemistry, detecting high concentrations of sulfur, chlorine, and bromine left behind by evaporating water.

★ Discovery: Confirmed the bright "Homestake" vein at Endeavour Crater was pure gypsum.
ROBOTIC ARM • OPTICS 30 μm / PIXEL

05. Microscopic Imager (MI)

A geologist's hand lens paired with a macro CCD camera, resolving fine sedimentary grains, cross-lamination ripples, and spherical concretions across 3 cm × 3 cm patches.

★ Discovery: Captured close-up textures of millimeter-scale Martian "blueberries".
ROBOTIC ARM • GRINDER 45 mm BORE

06. Rock Abrasion Tool (RAT)

Served as Opportunity's geological hammer: high-speed diamond-impregnated grinding wheels cut 45 mm circular holes up to 5 mm deep through weathered rock rinds.

★ Discovery: Ground into dozens of Martian rocks to expose pristine water-altered interiors.
